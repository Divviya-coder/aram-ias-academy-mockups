Dimensions

What the diagnostic measures.

Mains Diagnostic

  • Knowledge

    Conceptual accuracy and syllabus coverage.

  • Question Demand

    Ability to identify what the question is really asking.

  • Structure

    Introduction, body flow, transitions and conclusion.

  • Analysis

    Depth of argument, balance and critical thinking.

  • Value Addition

    Examples, data, case studies and contemporary linkage.

  • Execution

    Time management, legibility and presentation under pressure.

Prelims Diagnostic

  • Accuracy

    Correct recall and application.

  • Elimination

    Logical removal of incorrect options.

  • Recall

    Speed and reliability of factual retrieval.

  • Concept Gaps

    Topics where understanding is shallow.

  • PYQ Performance

    Pattern recognition across previous years.

  • Risk Behaviour

    Guessing patterns and decision-making under uncertainty.

Process

Five steps. One clear action plan.

1

Submit / Attempt

Answer a structured set of questions or upload recent answers.

2

Analyse

ARAM AI identifies patterns across the diagnostic dimensions.

3

Performance Map

You receive a visual snapshot of strengths and recurring errors.

4

Mentor Review

A mentor reviews the AI findings and adds clinical judgment.

5

Action Plan

You leave with a prioritised set of next actions and module recommendations.
