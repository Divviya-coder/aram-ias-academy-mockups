About ARAM

Aram for Students. Students for Aram.

A Chennai-built academic engine, now expanding into a national performance system for serious UPSC aspirants.

Story

The ARAM story.

Since 2011

ARAM began as a civil-services academy focused on UPSC, IFoS and TNPSC preparation. Over the years, it has built classroom programmes, tests, current-affairs products and in-house materials around serious aspirants.

Aram For Students

The ARAM identity is student-first: structured teaching, daily academic support, answer-writing practice, evaluation and mentorship that help aspirants move from preparation to performance.

Academic Engine

What ARAM has built.

Foundation Classes

UPSC and TNPSC classroom preparation with Prelims, Mains, CSAT and answer-writing orientation.

Current Affairs Desk

News Navigator, PIB analysis and Prediction Current Affairs Quiz turn daily events into exam-ready material.

Mains Writing

Answer-writing practice, examples, review and rewriting support for students moving from knowledge to marks.

Exclusive Materials

Mainframe, Do Not Miss, Rush Through and in-house publications support revision, enrichment and Mains application.

Digital Platform

Tests, resources, recorded content, dashboards and ARAM AI support learning beyond the classroom.

Results + Recognition

Selections, ranks, interviews and public recognitions show the visible proof of ARAM's academic system.
