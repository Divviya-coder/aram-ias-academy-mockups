Ethics Essay

Ethics

Concept → Dilemma → Decision.

Concept

Understand the ethical theory or value in the syllabus context.

Dilemma

Identify the conflict: competing duties, stakeholders, consequences.

Stakeholders

Map who is affected and in what way.

Competing Values

Articulate the tension between two legitimate values.

Options

Generate realistic courses of action.

Justification

Choose and defend the best option with reasoning.

Essay

Theme → Architecture → Synthesis.

Theme

Identify the central idea and its dimensions.

Dimensions

Break the theme into social, economic, political, ethical and historical angles.

Argument Architecture

Build a coherent structure with progression, not a list of points.

Examples

Use evidence, thinkers, contemporary cases and personal insight.

Transitions

Move smoothly between paragraphs and sections.

Synthesis

End with a balanced, forward-looking conclusion.

Practice

Evaluation example.

First Attempt

“Integrity is the most important value for a civil servant.”

Integrity is important because a civil servant must be honest. Without integrity, corruption will happen. Therefore, integrity is the most important value.

Issues: Circular argument, no stakeholder analysis, no counterpoint, no example.

After Framework Training

“Integrity is necessary but not sufficient...”

Integrity establishes trust between the state and citizen, but it must coexist with competence, empathy and accountability. A honest but ineffective officer can still fail the public. The question is whether integrity is the most important value, not the only one.

Improvements: Nuanced position, stakeholder awareness, balanced argument.
