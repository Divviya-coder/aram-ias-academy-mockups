The Difference

From marks to a learning loop.

Old Model

Write → Marks → Next Answer

The same weaknesses repeat because the diagnosis is hidden inside a score.

WriteLab Model

Write → Diagnose → Discuss → Rewrite → Compare → Track Pattern

Every answer becomes a chance to find and fix a specific pattern.

Evaluation

What WriteLab evaluates.

Demand Recognition

Did you answer the question that was asked?

Structure

Introduction, body flow, transitions and conclusion.

Relevance

Is every sentence serving the answer?

Analytical Depth

Balance, nuance and multi-dimensional thinking.

Evidence / Examples

Data, case studies, contemporary references.

Presentation

Legibility, spacing, subheadings and readability.

Conclusion

Forward-looking value, not summary.

Example

Original → Diagnosis → Rewrite.

Illustrative example based on typical WriteLab work.

Original

“Climate change is a big problem for India.”

Climate change is a big problem for India. It affects agriculture and health. The government should take steps. India should focus on renewable energy and adaptation.

Issues: Generic introduction, no demand recognition, no examples, weak conclusion.

Score: 3.5 / 10

Rewrite

“India's climate vulnerability is multidimensional...”

India's climate vulnerability is multidimensional: monsoon variability (IMD data), Himalayan glacial retreat and coastal exposure. The question demands both adaptation and mitigation, with federal coordination and state capacity building.

Improvements: Demand recognised, data cited, structure clear, balanced argument.

Score: 7.5 / 10
