Curriculum

What the programme covers.

GS I

History, geography, society and culture with integrated Prelims-Mains linkage.

GS II

Polity, governance, constitution and international relations.

GS III

Economy, environment, science & technology, security and disaster management.

GS IV

Ethics, integrity and aptitude with case-study practice.

Essay

Theme identification, argument architecture and structured writing practice.

CSAT

Comprehension, reasoning, quantitative aptitude and decision-making.

Learning Model

How the foundation is built.

Concept Classes

Structured lectures focused on conceptual clarity and syllabus coverage.

Tests

Regular Prelims and Mains tests to check understanding and build exam temperament.

Current Affairs

Daily linkage of news to static concepts and answer application.

Mentorship

Guidance on study planning, backlog management and exam strategy.

Platform

Access to recorded lectures, resources, tests and student dashboard.

Answer Writing

Writing practice starts early and intensifies as the programme progresses.
