Six principles we build around.

FAQ

Common questions.

What data does ARAM store?

Basic profile, diagnostic responses, answers, test attempts, mentor notes and platform interactions. We store only what is needed for learning and improvement.

Does AI replace faculty evaluation?

No. AI assists with pattern detection. Faculty and trained mentors make evaluative and academic decisions.

What sources does Ask ARAM use?

Approved ARAM study material, PYQs, current affairs produced by ARAM and faculty-verified reference content.

Can AI-generated questions enter tests automatically?

No. Any question entering a test or diagnostic is reviewed through the academic approval workflow.

How are student answers used?

For personalised feedback, pattern analysis and improvement tracking. They are not used for any purpose outside the learning system without consent.

How can a student request deletion or correction?

Contact privacy@aramiasacademy.com or use the data request form in your student account.
